abstract: "Occlusion culling is a cornerstone of real-time rendering, particularly in mobile games where limited GPU bandwidth demands highly efficient scene management. At the heart of occlusion culling lies the use of simplified proxy geometry, called occluders, that approximate scene geometry for rapid visibility testing. Producing high-quality occluders that are low in polygon count, conservative in coverage, and tightly aligned with the original geometry remains manual and labor-intensive. We present a fast, fully automated two-stage approach for robust occluder generation tailored to real-world game assets. The method combines a novel inward offset mesh strategy with conservative simplification using a new variant of Quadric Error Metrics. It handles noisy and topologically complex inputs, generates production-ready occluders in seconds, and achieves aggressive triangle reduction while preserving critical occlusion fidelity."
